Select a muscle group and goal to see a prescription.
Notes: Single-leg and double-leg variations aren't listed separately — pick the version you need, but master the double-leg version first. Dumbbells and kettlebells are largely interchangeable. Core-lower and upper/lower combination exercises should only be attempted once you're confident in each component movement on its own. Sets/reps/rest apply to whichever goal you select, regardless of exercise chosen.

Videos: matched automatically from the "Strength library" YouTube playlist by title. Some matches may be off, and some exercises don't have a video yet — use + add video on any exercise to search the full playlist and attach the right one, or hit the ✕ on a tab to remove a bad match. Your edits are saved in this browser only.

Building a workout: hit + add on any exercise to drop it into your workout. The guide's rule of thumb is up to 3 exercises per muscle, or up to 5 for a combined muscle group — you'll get a heads-up if you go over, but it won't stop you. When you're done, hit Create workout sheet for a clean, printable table with sets/reps/rest for each exercise at the goal you picked when adding it. Your workout is also saved in this browser only.
